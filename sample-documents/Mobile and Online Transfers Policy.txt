Sample Bank: Mobile and Online Transfers Policy

Section 1: Transfer limits
Mobile transfers are limited to EGP 30,000 per day. A customer can raise the limit to EGP 100,000 per day with written approval from their branch.

Section 2: Timing
Transfers between accounts at Sample Bank are immediate. Transfers to other banks in Egypt arrive by the next working day.

Section 3: Fees
Transfers between accounts at Sample Bank are free. Transfers to other banks in Egypt cost EGP 10 each.

Section 4: Failed transfers
If money leaves an account but does not reach the recipient, the customer should report it within 30 days. The bank investigates and returns the money within 7 working days if the transfer has failed.

Section 5: Security
Each transfer needs a one-time code sent to the customer's registered mobile number. Three wrong codes lock mobile banking for 24 hours.
