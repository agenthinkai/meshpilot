Sample Bank: Account Fees Policy

Section 1: Opening an account
Opening a current account requires a minimum deposit of EGP 5,000. Opening a savings account requires a minimum deposit of EGP 1,000.

Section 2: Monthly fees
The monthly management fee on a current account is EGP 25. The fee is waived for accounts with an average monthly balance above EGP 100,000. Savings accounts have no monthly fee.

Section 3: Statements
Electronic statements are free. A printed statement costs EGP 20 per page.

Section 4: Dormant accounts
An account with no customer transactions for 12 months becomes dormant. A dormant account can only be reactivated at a branch, with the customer's national ID. No fee applies for reactivation.

Section 5: Closing an account
A customer can close an account at any time after settling all obligations. There is no closing fee after the account has been open for 6 months. Closing it sooner costs EGP 100.
