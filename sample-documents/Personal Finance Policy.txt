Sample Bank: Personal Finance Policy

Section 1: Who can apply
Applicants must be between 21 and 60 years old, with at least 12 months in their current job.

Section 2: Amounts and terms
Personal finance ranges from EGP 20,000 to EGP 500,000, for a term of up to 60 months.

Section 3: Monthly payments
Monthly payments cannot exceed 40 percent of the applicant's monthly income.

Section 4: Decisions
The bank gives a decision within 5 working days of receiving all documents.

Section 5: Early settlement
A customer can settle early at any time. No early settlement fee applies after the first 12 months. Before that, the fee is 2 percent of the remaining amount.
