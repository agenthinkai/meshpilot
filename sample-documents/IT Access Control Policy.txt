Sample Bank: IT Access Control Policy

Section 1: Passwords
Staff passwords must have at least 14 characters and must be changed every 90 days. The last 10 passwords cannot be reused.

Section 2: Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access and for all administrator accounts.

Section 3: Access reviews
Managers review their team's system access every 6 months. Access for employees who leave is removed on their last working day.

Section 4: Audit logs
Access logs for core banking systems are kept for 2 years and reviewed by Information Security each month.

Section 5: External sharing
Staff must not upload customer data to external websites or public AI services.
