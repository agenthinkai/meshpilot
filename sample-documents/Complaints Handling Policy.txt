Sample Bank: Complaints Handling Policy

Section 1: How to complain
Customers can complain at any branch, through the contact centre, or through the mobile app.

Section 2: Response times
The bank acknowledges a complaint within 2 working days and sends a final answer within 15 working days. Complex cases can take up to 30 working days, and the customer must be told the reason in writing.

Section 3: Escalation
A customer who is not satisfied with the final answer can ask the Complaints Manager for a review within 60 days. The review takes up to 10 working days.

Section 4: Refunds
Fees charged by mistake are refunded within 30 days of the error being confirmed.

Section 5: Records
Complaint records are kept for 5 years after the complaint is closed.
