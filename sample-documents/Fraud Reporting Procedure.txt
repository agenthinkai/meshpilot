Sample Bank: Fraud Reporting Procedure

Section 1: Staff duty to report
Any employee who suspects fraud must report it to the Fraud Team on the same working day. Employees must not tell the customer involved.

Section 2: Customer reports
When a customer reports fraud, staff block the affected card or channel immediately and open a case within 1 hour.

Section 3: Investigation
The Fraud Team gives the customer a first update within 3 working days. Investigations are completed within 20 working days.

Section 4: Protecting staff
Employees who report fraud in good faith are protected from any retaliation.
